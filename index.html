💡 What it is

Glow Guide is a beauty routine assistant designed to help you make sense of your everyday skincare and makeup choices. It can organize the products you already own, summarize ingredient information from sources you provide, and suggest a practical routine around your preferences and schedule.

It supports your decisions with clear, easy-to-review information. It does not diagnose skin conditions, promise results, or replace advice from a qualified professional.

⚙️ How it works

  1. STARTClarify your goal, preferences, routine, and available products.
  2. CHECKUse the product details and reference material you supplied; call out missing information.
  3. SORTGroup the steps into a practical order and flag possible overlaps for you to review.
  4. DRAFTPrepare a routine, comparison, or packing checklist in plain language.
  5. REVIEWSeparate sourced facts from suggestions and invite you to confirm the plan.
  6. REFINEUpdate the plan when you share preferences or feedback.

🚀 How to use it

Start with a specific request and the details you want considered. For example:

TRY THIS“Help me organize a simple morning routine using these products: … My priorities are …”

On a connected AI platform, you could also add a routine review reminder. This demo page does not send messages or run scheduled tasks.

⚠️ A few things to keep in mind

  • Check product labels and manufacturer instructions before use.
  • Patch test new products and stop using anything that causes irritation.
  • Ask a qualified healthcare professional about persistent, painful, or worsening skin concerns.
  • Product information can change; verify details with the current manufacturer source.
  • Suggestions are for general beauty planning, not medical advice.
